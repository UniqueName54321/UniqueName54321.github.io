Hi. I am a growing (both literally and metaphorically) FOSS software creator and engineer. You might've seen my old account "UniqueName12345" lying around, but [https://github.com/UniqueName54321](https://github.com/UniqueName54321) is my new account.  
My older projects were rough learning experiments, and they no longer represent the standard I hold myself to. At the moment I'm working on Jaboticaba and LycaraOS (my grandest projects yet, no spoilers :P)

Just recently (as of writing this blog-post), I have been made aware of a project called "Ethical FOSS" (which I shall now occasionally refer to as Unethical POS) over on Codeberg (I will get back to this one later). From the name, I expected a project concerned with making FOSS communities and development practices more ethical. While they DO do that via their "Code Of Conduct", they don't focus on that. Rather, they seem to be exerting more effort into AI and why exactly it's allegedly "bad" for FOSS.

Full disclosure: I absolutely could not get remotely anywhere close to where I am today if it wasn't for AI (the very thing 'Ethical FOSS' is trying their best to 'fight' against), so I have a horse in this race. An artificial horse, yes, but one I have watched closely for years as the technology continued to evolve.

## Problem 1: "Open Slopware", and how it, in itself, is slop

Let's address the elephant in the room here - it is, effectively, a massive list of software that commits the absolutely heinous and execrable sin of "using new and capable technology". As such, naturally, it's not going to go over well to anyone who is outside the particular subset of FOSS advocates who regard any generative-AI involvement as disqualifying.

Not only that, but it's a project that [they themselves](https://codeberg.org/ethical-foss/open-slopware/src/branch/main/FAQ.md#why-is-removal-harder-than-landing-on-the-list-in-the-first-place) admit is a sticky blacklist from which projects can escape only by adopting the maintainers’ preferred ideology.
