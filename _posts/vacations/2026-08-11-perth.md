Okay, so I've decided to do something a bit different from my normal blog posts. I'm finally breaking free from any inhibitions I might've had about posting about my holidays online, and talking about one of my holidays for the first time. More specifically, my holiday up to Perth! 

Not the Perth in Scotland, the Perth in Australia!

No, not the [Perth in Tasmania either](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perth,_Tasmania). Look, let's just talk about my holiday in the Western Australian city of Perth and it's suburbs.

### The Trip To Perth

Every Perth trip (or, at least, one from *my* small town) has to start off with driving past Beaufort River and Arthur River. However, unlike some people, we couldn't resist the Dejaxo bakery over in Williams, so we *had* to make a stop there.

I was considering getting gelato, but then I ended up moving towards the smokie roll instead.
What *is* a smokie roll? Well, according to this bakery, it seems to be some "pigs in a blanket" variant involving cheese and bacon. According to Google, it seems to just straight up *be* "pigs in a blanket". Either way, it was apparantly so good that I had to pick it.

...but not good enough that I *took a photo of it*, so it only exists in my memory.

## Saturday, 31st July

### Breakfast

I had toast. This is going to be a repeating pattern throughout this entire holiday, methinks.

### Op Shops

I went to multiple op shops as the very first thing I go to. Interestingly, I got multiple books at a op shop unrelated to the one that said "book bazar" (where I got 0 books from). This either means that marketing is lying or that my taste in literature is abnormal.

### CREEC / Kent Street Weir

I then went on over to the Kent Street Weir, which is like this educational building where you learn stuff about the local wildlife of Kent Street. Some parts of it were pretty interesting, like the fact that (in addition to having taxidermies of stuff you'd expect like local birds) they also taxidermied some more unusual stuff like a red fox and even *a stray cat*.

#### The Walk

Kent Street Weir also has a walk attached. I naturally went on the walk, only to then find that the water beneath me had jellyfish in it. Yes, that's right.

![Jellyfish. Just, jellyfish.]({{ "/assets/images/perth/IMG_1119.jpg" | relative_url }})

### Carousel

#### Megaculture

The first stop I ever made was Megaculture, which was a massive shrine to all things anime (Yes, even *that* side of anime, according to a sign :P). At the time, I wanted to complete my collection of NGNL manga that I found the first volume at DSL Op Shop over in Albany (because my best friend in real life also has a manga collection, and imitation is the sincerest form of flattery).

However, as it turns out, NGNL was one of those weird manga where certain volumes are actually *banned* for sale in Australia. This would make it slightly more difficult to finish the collection, but what would make it truly difficult was that they were entirely sold out.

#### Daiso

I then went to a interesting concept for a store. Everything it sold was Japanese. Not like Megaculture, where it's specifically Japanese *animation* / *culture* (in fact, Daiso specifically doesn't sell Japanese culture for some reason), but rather where it was a normal shop but everything it sold happened to come from the country of Japan.

I'm talking cooking supplies from Japan, pans from Japan, toys from Japan, candy from Japan (which I actually did get by the way). This wasn't even the only shop in Perth to do this, but the other shop is for later.

### Speech

Did you know that I actually have speech therapy? Well, I went to my speechie in person. She's pretty nice. We usually do telehealth, but both parties could not resist being able to see each other in person.

The speechie has the option of a "Don't Break the Ice"-ish game, tic-tac-toe/chess/checkers in one (not nearly as fun as it sounds, it merely has the *option* of being any of those 3 at any given time) or another game I forgot. I picked the "Don't Break the Ice"-ish game.

#### Universes and Unrealities
